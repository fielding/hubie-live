# Ad break videos

Drop commercials here (mp4, webm or mov, up to 2 minutes). About once an hour Hubie announces a
break, plays the next one on his monitor with the music off and play paused, then comes back.
Set the interval and his lines in `[ad_break]` in `showrunner/config.toml`. No videos, no breaks.

Videos are gitignored: they stay on the machine that streams.
